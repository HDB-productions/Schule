# Fotografische Einzelsprites für die Animation

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug. Gespeichert als `ball-sprite.png` und `car-sprite.png`. Beide enthalten echte Alpha-Transparenz. Positionen, Bildfolge, Zeitraster, Rotation, Verblassen und Bewegungsunschärfe werden von JavaScript berechnet. Die alten generierten Gesamtbilder werden nicht mehr eingebunden.

## ball-sprite.png

Use case: scientific-educational. One single photorealistic yellow basketball isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Entire perfectly round sphere, centered tightly cropped, ball diameter90% of square canvas. Detailed yellow orange rubber pebble texture and distinct thin curved dark basketball seams, directional studio lighting upper left, believable shading. No shadow outside ball, no ground, no drop shadow, NO motion blur. This will rotate in a scientific animation. Exactly ONE ball, no text, no frame, no graphics.

## car-sprite.png

Use case: scientific-educational. One single realistic red compact hatchback TOY CAR, perfect exact side profile, facing right, isolated on genuinely transparent background. Photographic polished red paint, black window trim, dark glass, realistic rubber tires with visible silver spokes, no brand logos. TWO wheels completely visible, front wheel center about82% car length, rear about20%. Car body horizontal, entire vehicle visible, tight rectangular crop with very small transparent margin, aspect ratio about2.6:1. Studio lighting upper left. No ground or shadow outside vehicle. NO motion blur, no reflections outside vehicle, no extra views, no words. This is an individual photographic sprite for a multiple exposure science animation. Same visual style as a red compact model Volkswagen-like hatchback, no logo.
